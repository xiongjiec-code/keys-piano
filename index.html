How it works: put your phone or tablet on the piano's music stand. The sheet music scrolls past the blue line, and each note turns green when the app hears you play it. Any octave counts.

Each lesson has three steps, one star each: ① Learn waits for you on every note. ② Slow and ③ Full speed keep the music moving, so you play along in time.

The app makes no sound. The small key map under the music only shows where the note is; turn it off with Guide.
